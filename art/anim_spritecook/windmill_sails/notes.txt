Windmill sails turning. 8 frames, 240x224 each, frame_00 = the original painted sails exactly (sails.png is the same as frame_00).
Hub (rotation centre) at pixel (120,112) of each frame. Put the sprite origin there and draw it at the tower sprite's (80,68), i.e. frame top-left at tower (-40,-44). Draw the sails above the tower.
The 8 frames cover 90 degrees (the four sails repeat every quarter turn), so frame_07 -> frame_00 loops seamlessly. Turning anticlockwise on screen; play in reverse for clockwise. Suggested 6-8 fps for a lazy turn.
Made locally (no credits): the sails were cut from the original windmill and rotated in a 'flattened' space - the painted X is foreshortened (arms at +-40 deg, not 45), so each frame un-squashes y by 1.19, rotates 11.25 deg, squashes back, at 8x supersampling with majority-colour downsampling. Alternative: rotate sails.png in-engine around (120,112) - it will look slightly off since the art is foreshortened.
