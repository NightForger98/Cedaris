# Ranged weapons: the boomerang flies out, turns back and is caught (it isn't in your hand meanwhile, and can't be
# thrown again until it's back); the bows are carried with their walk_128 sheet and shoot arrows (one used a shot).
start story
wait 60
time 12
pos 600 470
give c|held|Boomerang
equip c|held|Boomerang
face e
wait 10
use
snap 30 boomerang_out
call autotest_boomerang_surface
call autotest_shots_where
wait 20
call boomerang_out
expect 1
wait 30
shot boomerang_far
call autotest_shots_where
wait 60
shot boomerang_back
wait 60
call boomerang_out
expect 0
give c|held|Bow
give m|arrow 5
equip c|held|Bow
face s
wait 10
shot bow_standing
keydown right
wait 14
shot bow_walking
keyup right
wait 10
face e
use
snap 40 bow_shot
wait 60
inv
expect m|arrowx4
quit
