# Weapon charges have three levels (the glow, the red face, the anger mark), each hitting harder and knocking further
# (docs/CHARGING.md): a sword slashes wide at 1 (not behind you), spins all round at 2 and 3; a mace swings a wider
# arc in front at each level (never behind you); the top level knocks far and stuns; a bow's arrow pierces one at 2.
start story
wait 60
gold 1000
# in the mine, on a stretch of floor open for 380 px to the right (on the farm, fences and trees stopped the arrows)
mine 1
wait 40
call autotest_open_floor 120 380 90
expectnot none
# strong enough for iron weapons (scripts/skills: metal gear needs Strength)
call autotest_attr strength 10
give c|held|Arming Sword
give c|held|Mace
give c|held|Bow
give m|arrow 20
equip c|held|Arming Sword
call autotest_monster_ring slime 26
equip c|held|Arming Sword
face e
call autotest_monster_ring slime 26
call autotest_toughen
charge 1
wait 30
call autotest_monster_hits
expect hit 3 of 4
log Arming Sword level 1
equip c|held|Arming Sword
face e
call autotest_monster_ring slime 26
call autotest_toughen
charge 2
wait 30
call autotest_monster_hits
expect hit 4 of 4
log Arming Sword level 2
equip c|held|Arming Sword
face e
call autotest_monster_ring slime 26
call autotest_toughen
charge 3
wait 30
call autotest_monster_hits
expect hit 4 of 4
call autotest_knocked_over 40

call autotest_monsters_stunned
expect stunned
log Arming Sword level 3
equip c|held|Mace
face e

charge 1
